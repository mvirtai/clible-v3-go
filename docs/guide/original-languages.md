# Original Languages & Morphology

The **Original Language Study View** (`/original`) provides scholars, pastors, and language enthusiasts with deep morphological and lexical analysis of biblical Koine Greek (New Testament) and Biblical Hebrew (Old Testament).

---

## 1. Source Texts & Language Packs

clible-v3 supports standard critical original language source texts:

- **Koine Greek**: SBL Greek New Testament (`greeksblgnt`).
- **Biblical Hebrew**: Aleppo Codex and Leningrad Codex Hebrew text (`hebrewaleppocodex`).

When you select a scripture passage, the system automatically detects whether the target reference belongs to the Old Testament (Hebrew) or the New Testament (Greek) and activates the appropriate source language engine.

## 2. Interlinear Breakdown & Morphological Tags

For each word in the source text, the interlinear analysis provides:

1. **Original Script & Transliteration**: Clear Greek/Hebrew typography with phonetic transliteration.
2. **Lemma (Dictionary Form)**: The uninflected root form of the word.
3. **Part of Speech & Grammatical Tags**:
   - **Nouns / Adjectives**: Case (Nominative, Genitive, Dative, Accusative, Vocative), Gender (Masc, Fem, Neut), Number (Sg, Pl).
   - **Verbs**: Tense (Present, Aorist, Imperfect, Perfect, Pluperfect, Future), Voice (Active, Middle, Passive), Mood (Indicative, Subjunctive, Imperative, Infinitive, Participle), Person, and Number.
4. **Lexical Semantic Range**: Comprehensive definition and translation glosses.

---

## 3. Contextual Exegesis & Root Studies

Beyond word-by-word breakdowns, the view synthesizes original language observations:

- **Syntax & Word Order Insights**: Explains significant stylistic choices (such as fronting of emphatic words or chiastic structures).
- **Theological Keyword Investigation**: Explores words with rich doctrinal backgrounds (such as *Hesed*, *Agape*, *Dikaiosyne*, *Shalom*).
- **Next Focus Recommendations**: Interactive chips offering contextual study avenues (e.g. *Investigate Johannine Logos concept*, *Compare with Genesis 1:1 Hebrew Bereshit*).
- **Deep-Dive Exegesis Cards**: Expandable comprehensive study notes covering historical-grammatical exegesis.
